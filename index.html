Got Questions? We Have Answers!

Any modern Chromebook, Windows PC, Mac, or tablet with an updated Google Chrome or Safari browser will work! For Scratch block coding, tablets like iPads work great. For Python and HTML/CSS web coding, a keyboard is strongly recommended for comfortable typing.
Kids can begin as early as 7 or 8 with our visual Scratch track, which focuses on spatial logic, drag-and-drop mechanics, and playful storytelling. Once they hit 10 or 11 and can comfortably type sentences, our Web Magic and Python tracks are ideal next steps.
